# Residential Electrical Panel Retrofit

I designed and built this residential distribution board as a complete panel overhaul. The project involved laying out the component architecture, planning a compliant protection scheme, and executing clean, structured wiring across two DIN rails.

## Design & Standards Compliance (ΕΛΟΤ HD 384)

The board's architecture and safety measures were designed according to the **Greek Standard ΕΛΟΤ HD 384** (*Requirements for Electrical Installations*):

* **Centralized RCD Protection:** In accordance with ΕΛΟΤ HD 384 rules for residential safety, the entire board is protected by a the means of a Residual Current Device. Operating at IΔₙ = 30 mA, it provides essential protection against indirect contact, insulation faults, and earth leakage across all downstream branch circuits.
* **Overcurrent & Short-Circuit Protection:** Individual single-pole Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCBs) protect downstream lighting, general sockets, and dedicated high-power appliance circuits.


## Assembly & Wiring Execution

Physical assembly strictly followed **ΕΛΟΤ HD 384** specification guidelines:

* **Standardized Color Coding:** Adhered strictly to national standards for conductors—Brown/Black for Phase (L), Blue for Neutral (N), and Green/Yellow for Protective Earth (PE).
* **Conductor Sizing & Selectivity:** Cable cross-sectional areas (1.5 mm², 2.5 mm², and 6 mm²+ jumper lines) were accurately selected based on MCB ampacity ratings to prevent conductor overheating.

## Testing & Commissioning

Before energization, pre-commissioning verification was carried out as mandated by **ΕΛΟΤ HD 384**:

* **Continuity & Earth Loop Testing:** Confirmed low-resistance end-to-end ground continuity on all protective conductors (PE).
* **Insulation Resistance (IR):** Verified high dielectric integrity between Live–Neutral, Live–Earth, and Neutral–Earth lines.
* **RCD Trip Testing:** Tested the single RCD's trip currents (IΔₙ) and response times using an RCD tester to ensure guaranteed fault clearing.
