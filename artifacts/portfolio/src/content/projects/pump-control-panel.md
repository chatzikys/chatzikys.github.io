# Pump Control Panel

I designed and built two identical motor control panels for pump applications using a star–delta starter configuration to reduce starting current and ensure smooth motor operation.

## Overview

Star–delta starting is a common technique for reducing the inrush current of three-phase induction motors during startup. By initially connecting the motor windings in a star (Y) configuration, the voltage across each winding is reduced by a factor of √3, limiting the starting current to approximately one-third of the direct-on-line value. After the motor reaches near-rated speed, the contactors switch to a delta (Δ) configuration for normal operation.

## Panel Design

Each panel included:
- **Main circuit breaker** for overcurrent and short-circuit protection
- **Star and delta contactors** with mechanical and electrical interlocking
- **Timer relay** to control the Y–Δ transition sequence
- **Thermal overload relay** for motor protection
- **Indicator lights** for power, run, and fault status

## Wiring & Assembly

Both panels were wired from scratch following all relevant standards, including:
- Proper cable sizing for the motor rating
- Numbered terminal blocks for structured wiring
- DIN rail mounting of all components

## Result

Both panels were successfully tested, demonstrating smooth startup with reduced current draw and reliable transition from star to delta configuration.
